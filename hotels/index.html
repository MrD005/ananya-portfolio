TravClan · Hotel booking

Helping agents choose hotels with confidence.

A clearer hotel discovery and room-selection experience that keeps the information agents need close to every booking decision.

Role
Product designer
Scope
Portal redesign
Platform
B2B web app
Focus
Hotel selection
NDA note

01

Overview

The challenge

Hotel decisions carried too much information and too little clarity.

Travel agents needed to evaluate a property, understand room options and track the booking total without losing their place in the flow.

The redesign focused on making dense hotel information easier to scan while preserving the operational detail agents rely on.

The objective was not simply a visual refresh. It was to create a more coherent decision path from property evaluation to room selection and booking.

Design question

How might we reduce comparison effort while keeping booking context visible?

02

Research

What guided the redesign

Understand where agents hesitate before changing the interface.

Research findings, market patterns and competitor analysis were used to frame the most important moments in the existing hotel-selection journey.

01

Information hierarchy

Property details, facilities, policies and location needed clearer levels of importance.

02

Room comparison

Agents needed to compare inclusions, policies and prices without scanning disconnected screens.

03

Booking context

Selections and totals had to remain visible while agents evaluated alternatives.

04

Decision confidence

Reviews, facilities and cancellation terms needed to appear where they could influence a choice.

03

Approach

From evidence to interface

Turn a dense booking workflow into a sequence of focused decisions.

  1. 01

    Synthesize user signals

    Combine user feedback, observed pain points, market patterns and competitor behavior.

  2. 02

    Prioritize the decision points

    Define which property, room, policy and pricing details matter at each stage of selection.

  3. 03

    Prototype the connected flow

    Translate the information model into screens that preserve context between hotel and room choices.

  4. 04

    Validate and refine

    Use feedback from users and cross-functional partners to improve clarity and implementation readiness.

04

Design

The final direction

Keep the property, the options and the total in the same conversation.

The interface uses a persistent summary to anchor the booking while the main workspace changes from property details to rooms and alternative hotels.

05

Outcome & learning

What I took forward

Dense products feel simpler when context stays put.

The resulting direction connected property evaluation, room comparison and booking review without forcing agents to reconstruct their choices at every step.

The work reinforced that progressive disclosure is most useful when the essential summary remains visible and stable.
